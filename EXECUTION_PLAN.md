## SUCCESS/FAILURE CRITERIA

### MVP Beta is SUCCESS if:
- ✅ 100 beta users onboarded by Feb 1
- ✅ $2K-5K MRR by Feb 28
- ✅ <10% churn rate
- ✅ NPS score >50
- ✅ 50+ testimonials
- ✅ 5+ case studies
- ✅ 99.5% uptime
- ✅ <4 hour support response
- ✅ Lighthouse >90
- ✅ Zero critical security issues

### MVP Beta is FAILURE if:
- ❌ <50 beta users by Feb 1
- ❌ <$1K MRR by Feb 28
- ❌ >20% churn rate
- ❌ NPS score <30
- ❌ Uptime <95%
- ❌ Critical security vulnerability
- ❌ Customer satisfaction <3/5
- ❌ Major competitors launch feature parity

### Decision: If FAILURE on Feb 1
1. Extend beta to Mar 15
2. Use Feb as runway to fix critical issues
3. Revisit metrics on Mar 15
4. Decide: Continue to GA or pivot
